For a first milestone, I'd suggest [SCOPED OUTPUT] for [FIXED PRICE OR HOURLY ESTIMATE]. ## Do not do
- Do not claim you've audited or found defects in someone else's site without authorization.
- Do not exaggerate employment history or fabricate reviews, testimonials, customers, or results.
- Do not send high-volume generic spam or collect credentials by ordinary email.
