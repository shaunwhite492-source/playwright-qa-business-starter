# $249 Focused Web App QA Audit

## Who it's for
Small SaaS teams and agencies that want a second set of eyes on a release-critical workflow, without a long engagement.

## Included (fixed scope)
- One client-authorized public or staging web app.
- Up to **three agreed critical user journeys** (e.g. sign-up, onboarding, account settings).
- One desktop browser: Chromium; one standard desktop viewport.
- Up to **two hours** of structured exploratory/functional testing, plus up to **one hour** preparing the report.
- A concise pass/fail workflow summary, up to eight documented findings **if discovered**, priority/severity, reproduction steps, screenshots when safe to capture, and recommended next steps.
- One round of written clarifications within five business days of delivery.

## Not included
Bug fixes, building automated tests, security/penetration testing, load/performance testing, accessibility compliance certification, mobile/browser matrix testing, or exhaustive regression coverage. No guarantee that defects will be found or that the product is defect-free.

## Workflow
1. Client confirms in writing which environments and accounts may be tested and that they have authority to authorize the work. No scanning, scraping, or intrusive testing.
2. Agree on three flows, target URLs, credentials for **test-only** accounts through a secure channel, and known limitations.
3. Sign a short scope agreement. Pay $249 upfront or under a mutually agreed invoice schedule.
4. Execute tests, document evidence with sensitive information redacted, send PDF/Markdown report securely.
5. Deliver within **three business days after access and scope are confirmed**, subject to scheduling in the agreement.

## Optional follow-on
If manual testing reveals repeatable release risks, quote Playwright automation separately, e.g. a $900–$1,500 scoped starter project. No pressure and no assumed upsell.

## Economics
A three-hour delivery cap makes $249 about $83/hour **gross before client acquisition, taxes, payment fees, and business overhead**. Re-scope or quote separately if the client requests additional coverage.
